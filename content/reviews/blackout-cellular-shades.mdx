Across 403 verified ratings and a 4.6 out of 5 average, the buyer data suggests the product mostly delivers — with one structural limitation that matters.

The short version: this is a Buy for renters and homeowners who need blackout cellular shades without drilling into the window frame. For shift workers or nurseries that need total darkness, it is a gap worth knowing about before ordering a custom product that cannot be returned for sizing reasons.

## Common Complaints Worth Knowing

The complaint data clusters around three issues, two related to the tension-mount design and one to logistics.

First, cordless mechanism degradation. A subset of verified buyers report that the free-stop lift system loses consistency over time. The shade may stop holding its position, drift downward, raise unevenly, or bunch to one side. This is a known failure mode across cordless cellular shades from multiple manufacturers — not a Sulugood-specific defect — but it is the most common complaint in the critical reviews. The [Fix My Blinds troubleshooting guide](https://www.fixmyblinds.com/blogs/how-to-guides) confirms that resetting the internal spring tension by fully extending and then retracting the shade resolves the issue in most cases, though it may recur with continued daily use.

Second, edge light leakage. The tension-mount system holds the shade inside the window recess but does not seal it against the frame. Light enters around the top, bottom, and sides — the "halo effect" that affects all standard cellular shades installed without side channels. Buyers who expected total darkness report this as a significant gap between the marketing claim and the actual result. The shade fabric itself is fully opaque, but the installation method does not provide the edge seal that a screw-mounted system with integrated side tracks would offer. Select Blinds' Click2Fit system with built-in side channels is one alternative that addresses this, though it typically costs more and requires a slightly different installation approach.

Third, shipping and returns. The Sulugood is manufactured in China and ships directly, with a typical delivery window of 1.5 to 2 weeks. Custom-sized orders cannot be returned for measurement errors — if the buyer's measurements are wrong, the shade does not fit, and the purchase is lost. The manufacturer's guidance to measure in multiple spots (top, middle, and bottom of the frame) is sound, but buyers unfamiliar with window measurement tolerances still report ordering the wrong size. This is a risk specific to custom-made products and worth accounting for.

## Who Should Skip It

Buyers who need absolute, total blackout with zero light leakage around the edges should look at screw-mounted systems with integrated side channels instead. The Sulugood's tension-mount design leaves gaps at the frame perimeter that no amount of custom sizing can close. For shift workers sleeping during daylight hours or home theatre setups where any ambient light is unacceptable, this product will not deliver the result the "100% blackout" label suggests. The blackout claim applies to the fabric opacity, not to the installed system's light seal.

Anyone with non-standard window frames — arched, bevelled, textured, or with a recess depth under 1.4 inches — should verify compatibility before ordering. The rubber stopper tension system requires a smooth, flat surface to grip against, and the frame must be deep enough to accommodate the shade mechanism inside the recess. Textured plaster, rounded trim, or shallow frames reduce the security of the mount and increase the risk of the shade falling. Because custom orders are non-returnable, a measurement and compatibility check before ordering is not optional — it is the difference between a working installation and an expensive mistake.


## Sulugood vs Grandekor vs Persilux: How They Compare

### Sulugood vs Grandekor No-Drill Cordless Blackout Cellular Shades

The Grandekor uses a similar cordless, no-drill design with a high-load-bearing adsorption and spring system for tension mounting. Both products use polyester honeycomb fabric with blackout capability. The Sulugood differentiates with custom sizing to exact measurements and a dual anti-slip head with polymer coating, while the Grandekor typically ships in standard sizes that may require the buyer to accept a less precise fit. The Grandekor's adsorption-based mounting may hold more securely on certain frame types, but buyer data on long-term hold strength is similar across both brands. For exact-fit requirements, the Sulugood's custom sizing is the stronger option. For buyers comfortable with standard sizing, the Grandekor is a viable and often faster-shipping alternative.

### Sulugood vs Persilux Cordless Cellular Blackout Shades

The Persilux offers a feature the Sulugood does not: top-down/bottom-up operation. This allows the shade to be lowered from the top while remaining raised from the bottom, providing privacy at eye level while letting natural light in from above. Both products use 1-inch honeycomb cells and pressure-mounted tension systems. The Persilux is available in both standard and custom sizes. For buyers who want flexible light control — partial privacy without full blackout — the Persilux's dual-direction operation is a meaningful advantage. For buyers whose primary need is full blackout in a custom-fit, no-drill package, the Sulugood's aluminum-lined cells and dual anti-slip system are the more relevant specs.
